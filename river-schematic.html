Reading the diagram. Bar width is proportional to flow. Dashed red means the gage read zero — or didn't report — at fetch time. Angostura, Isleta, and San Acacia can each take the entire river at low flow.

Who's who. MRGCD is the Middle Rio Grande Conservancy District, the irrigation district running the diversion dams and canals. ABCWUA is the Albuquerque Bernalillo County Water Utility Authority, the municipal utility whose treatment plant discharges back to the river below Albuquerque. EBID is the Elephant Butte Irrigation District.

Method. These are the diverting agencies' own published numbers, taken at face value. Nobody — the districts included — tracks how much of a diversion's return flow gets rediverted downstream. The point is that the river gets captured repeatedly, in both Colorado and New Mexico, under two different legal regimes. This is not a mass balance.

Sources. MRGCD OneRain telemetry · USGS National Water Information System · USBR HydroData. Refreshed twice daily; USBR figures are the most recent day published, typically one day behind.
